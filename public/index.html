    User Guide

    Complete documentation for Data Extractor

    🚀 Getting Started

    Welcome to Data Extractor! This comprehensive tool helps you collect business leads and execute multi-channel outreach campaigns. Use the sidebar to navigate between different modules.

    Tip: Configure your Settings (Proxy, SMTP, Twilio) before running large campaigns to ensure smooth operation.

    📊 Extraction Tools

    1. Domain Extraction

    Upload a list of websites to extract comprehensive data including contact info and technology stack.

    • Data Points: Emails, Phone Numbers, Social Links, Meta Tags, Tech Stack.
    • Deep Crawl: Navigates internal pages (About, Contact, Team) to find hidden data.

    2. Google Maps Scraper

    Generate leads by finding local businesses based on keywords and location.

    • Keywords: Enter "Service in Location" (e.g., "Real Estate in New York").
    • Data: Business Name, Address, Phone, Website, Reviews, Ratings.
    • Email Discovery: Automatically visits business websites to find emails.

    3. LinkedIn Sales Navigator

    Extract professional profiles and job listings.

    • People Search: Find contacts by job title, company, experience level.
    • Session Management: Securely manage LinkedIn sessions for safe extraction.

    📢 Marketing & Outreach

    📧 Email Marketing

    Send personalized HTML emails to your leads using your own SMTP server.

    • Personalization: Use variables like {{name}}, {{company}} in templates.
    • Tracking: Real-time monitoring of sent status.

    📱 SMS Campaigns

    Reach leads directly via SMS using Twilio integration.

    • Bulk Sending: High-throughput SMS dispatch.
    • Compliance: Built-in rate limiting options.

    💬 WhatsApp Marketing

    Automate WhatsApp communication via WhatsApp Web.

    • Bulk Sender: Send messages to a list of numbers with attachment support.
    • Group Tools: Auto-join groups and extract member lists.

    ✅ Verification Tools

    Ensure your data is high quality before reaching out.

    • Email Verification: Verify validity via ZeroBounce, SMTP, or MX check.
    • WhatsApp Check: Bulk check if phone numbers are registered on WhatsApp.

    🛠️ Visual Tools

    Bulk Screenshots

    Capture visual snapshots of websites for verification or portfolio building.

    • Device Emulation: Capture as Desktop, Mobile (iPhone), or Tablet.

    🚦 Website Traffic Tester

    Generate realistic, automated traffic to any website for testing or SEO purposes.

    • Rotational Proxies: Uses your configured proxies to mask traffic sources.
    • Human Simulation: Mimics mouse movements and scrolling behavior.
    • Device Mixing: Randomly switches between Mobile and Desktop user agents.
    • Internal Visits: Bot will click internal links to reduce bounce rate.

    💾 Code Downloader

    Download the complete source code of a website for offline analysis or backup.

    • Render JavaScript: Uses a real browser to capture content rendered by React/Vue/Angular.
    • Single File: Saves the HTML structure ready for inspection.

    ℹ️ How are Lead Scores calculated?

    We grade each lead from 0-100 based on data completeness:

    • 📞 Phone Number: +20 points
    • 🌐 Website: +20 points
    • 📧 Email Address: +15 points
    • ⭐ Excellent Rating (4.5+): +15 points
    • 💬 High Reviews (100+): +10 points
    • ✅ Email Verified: +5 points
    • 📱 WhatsApp Available: +5 points
    Example: A business with a Phone, Website, and Email gets:
    20 + 20 + 15 = 55 points (Grade C).
