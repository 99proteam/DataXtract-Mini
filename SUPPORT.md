# Support

Use the right channel so questions can be answered efficiently:

- **Bug:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/99proteam/DataXtract-Mini/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Feature request:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/99proteam/DataXtract-Mini/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Setup or usage question:** start a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/99proteam/DataXtract-Mini/discussions).
- **Security issue:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.
- **Commercial product support:** email `support@bundlewp.com` or visit [BundleWP](https://bundlewp.com).

Include your operating system, Node.js version, browser version, relevant logs with secrets removed, and exact reproduction steps.
